# Code of Conduct

Dandelion Lite welcomes respectful participation from node operators, developers, researchers, students and community members, regardless of experience or background.

## Expected behavior

Be constructive and considerate. Critique ideas and code rather than people. Respect others' time, production constraints and differing technical perspectives. Help newcomers find documentation and discuss disagreements with evidence and patience.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualized or abusive conduct, deliberate disruption, publishing private information without permission, and attempts to pressure or intimidate contributors are not acceptable.

## Reporting and moderation

For ordinary disagreements, use the relevant GitHub thread or raise the concern privately with the core maintainer [Adriano Fiorenza (`zxpectre`)](https://github.com/zxpectre) via the [GameChanger Finance Discord](https://discord.gg/vpbfyRaDKG). If the concern involves that maintainer, contact a GameChanger Finance Discord server moderator instead. Do not publish sensitive personal details. Vulnerability reports belong under [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Project maintainers or community moderators may ask participants to change behavior, moderate comments, close threads or restrict participation. Reports will be handled discreetly where reasonably possible; no specific response-time guarantee is made. Discord moderators govern their own venue.

This policy applies to project repositories, official project discussions and project-hosted community interactions.
